# Privacy Policy for Asala Calendar

Last updated: June 1, 2026

Asala Calendar ("Asala", "the app") is an offline calendar app for Android,
developed by Arishawke. This policy explains what the app does and does not do
with your information.

## Summary

Asala collects no personal data. It contains no ads, no analytics, and no
trackers. It declares no internet permission, so it is technically incapable of
sending any information off your device. Nothing you enter into Asala leaves
your phone through the app.

## Information the app accesses

All access below happens locally on your device. None of it is collected,
transmitted, or shared by Asala.

- **Calendar events.** Asala reads and writes your events through Android's
  system calendar (the Calendar Provider). It keeps no separate database of its
  own. These are the same events every other calendar app on your device can
  see. If you have set up a sync account yourself (for example Google, Exchange,
  or CalDAV through the DAVx5 app), the syncing of those events is performed by
  Android and that account provider, not by Asala, and is governed by their
  privacy policies.
- **Reminders.** Notifications and reminder alarms are scheduled locally on your
  device.
- **Settings.** Your preferences (theme, default reminders, and similar) are
  stored locally on your device.

## Permissions and why the app requests them

- **Read and write calendar** to show and edit your events.
- **Notifications** to display event reminders.
- **Exact alarms** to fire reminders at the precise time you set.
- **Run after restart** to restore your scheduled reminders after the phone
  reboots.

## Data collection and sharing

Asala collects no data and shares no data. There is no account to create and
nothing is sent to the developer or any third party.

## Children

Asala is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
